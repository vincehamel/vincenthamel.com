Approach

Understand, decide, deliver.

An engagement moves in three stages. I stay involved until the results are there, not just until the report.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    Read the numbers, meet the team, pin down what really matters. A clear picture within a few weeks.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Easy-to-read models, costed options and candid recommendations that management, the board and your partners can stand behind.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    Negotiations, implementation, follow-through. I work with your team and advisors until the job is done.

Engagements that adapt to you

Every engagement is shaped around the needs of the company and its leaders, when you need it. We adjust along the way: more hands-on during a financing or a transaction, lighter once things are in place.

  • Project mandateA defined goal: a financing, a model, a transaction.
  • Ongoing advisoryA few days a month or a week, alongside leadership.
  • InterimA sustained presence during a transition or a critical period.
