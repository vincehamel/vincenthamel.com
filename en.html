Services

How I can help

A pragmatic, collaborative approach: I work alongside your team and your board, put the numbers to work for decisions, coordinate your outside advisors, and stay involved through execution.

  • Strategic advisor and fractional CFO

    An outside perspective and hands-on experience alongside leadership, without the cost of a full-time hire.

    • Support to the CEO and leadership team
    • Board reporting and governance
    • Coordination of legal and tax advisors

  • Financing and lender relations

    Finding the right capital, structuring it and negotiating it, from preparing the file through to closing.

    • Secured debt, credit, convertible debt, grants
    • Bringing in a strategic or financial equity partner
    • Debt renegotiation and covenant monitoring

  • Transactions, buyouts and succession

    Supporting owners through an acquisition, sale, shareholder buyout or change in ownership.

    • Valuation, normalized EBITDA and offer strategy
    • Letter of intent, negotiation and closing
    • Succession planning

  • Turnaround and restructuring

    Steady support when cash gets tight and decisions can't wait.

    • Tight cash management
    • Negotiations with multiple lenders and partners
    • Turnaround plan and return to growth

  • Finance structure, reporting and systems

    Reliable numbers and credible forecasts, produced by a well-organized team and toolset.

    • Budgets, cash flow forecasts and financial models
    • Finance team structure and month-end close
    • Standing up an independent back office, ERP

  • Operations and growth

    Connecting finance and operations to improve margins and accelerate revenue growth.

    • Business model, pricing and profitability
    • Commercialization and distribution partnerships
    • Commercial contract negotiation

Frequently asked questions

Before we start

What is a fractional CFO?

An experienced chief financial officer who works with your company a few days a month or a week, depending on your needs. You get the judgment and network of an executive-level CFO without the cost of a full-time position.

What kind of companies do you work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses and growing companies, especially in the creative industries, entertainment, culture, agencies, architecture and design, as well as non-profits and charities.

How does a first engagement work?

It starts with a 30-minute conversation, with no commitment, to understand your situation. I then send you a written engagement proposal that sets out the objectives, timeline, my role and fees.

Can you support a financing or transaction from start to finish?

Yes. I prepare the file, the financial model and the strategy, take part in negotiations with lenders, investors or buyers, and coordinate lawyers and tax advisors through to closing.

Do you work in English and French, and remotely?

Yes, in both languages. I am based in Montreal and work on site or remotely with companies in Quebec, elsewhere in Canada and in the United States.
