Modules

One system, from the hour worked to the financial statements.

Every piece of information is entered once and flows through: the hour becomes an invoice, the invoice revenue, the revenue a margin by client. Turn on only the modules you need.

  • Time

    Simple timesheets, a Friday reminder and hours suggested from your calendar.

  • Billing

    Rate cards, projects, freelancers, invoices in the client's language, wire and Interac.

  • Profitability and reports

    True margin by client and project, and your own reports in a few clicks, in Excel or PDF.

  • Expense reports

    Snap or email the receipt and AI reads it. Mileage, per diem and reimbursement.

  • Vendor payments

    Payment runs prepared and approved, with files ready for your bank.

  • Banking and reconciliation

    Imported statements, suggested matches, reusable rules, signed reconciliations.

  • Accounting and financial statements

    General ledger, recurring entries, balance sheet and income statement with comparatives. Or keep your accounting software.

  • Approvals and controls

    Approval workflows, a tamper-proof log, two-factor sign-in and role-based access.

Coming soon: client records and CRM · proposals and contracts with e-signature · GST and QST returns

What sets Simone apart

Built to run a business, not just keep the books.

Simone replaces the usual patchwork: accounting software, a time tracker, an expense app and a lot of Excel.

  • True profitability

    The margin on every client, using the fully loaded hourly cost: salary, benefits and overhead.

  • Built-in controls

    Approvals, adjustable segregation of duties and a tamper-proof log, from day one.

  • Made for Quebec

    Bilingual from day one, GST and QST, Canadian bank files and Law 25.

  • AI with guardrails

    An assistant that prepares the work, but never pays or contacts a client without your approval.
